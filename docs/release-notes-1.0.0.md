# TaskHero for Anki 1.0.0

The first release of TaskHero for Anki brings study rewards to Anki **26.8.1 or
later** on **Windows, macOS, and Linux**. Requires a TaskHero account.

- Earn 1–3 TaskHero points for each batch of at least 10 reviews.
- Complete your daily study habit after your chosen number of batches.
- See your progress while reviewing.
- Earn credit for mobile reviews after syncing to your connected computer.

See the [setup and everyday-use guide](../README.md) to connect and choose your
rewards.

## Known limitations

- Use one connected computer per synced collection to avoid duplicate rewards.
- Sync before connecting or reconnecting. Only history already on your computer
  is excluded; mobile reviews arriving later can still earn credit.
- Undo within ten seconds prevents a review's reward. Redo doesn't restore that
  credit, and rewards already sent can't be reversed from Anki.
- Replacing a deleted or ineligible habit doesn't recover a missed completion
  from earlier that day.
- A crash while rewards are being recorded can miss some reward credit.
  Rewards already saved for delivery retry after restarting. Your Anki study
  history is unaffected.

Your study content stays in Anki. See [Privacy](../PRIVACY.md) for details.
