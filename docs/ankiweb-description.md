# TaskHero for Anki

Turn your Anki reviews into TaskHero rewards.

Earn points as you study and complete your daily study habit when you reach
your goal. With the default settings, you earn 1 point for every 10 reviews and
complete your linked habit after 30 reviews. Choose your own batch size,
1–3 points per batch, and daily batch goal.

Again, Hard, Good, and Easy all count. Your progress appears in Anki while you
review. Study on AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid too—rewards arrive after syncing to your
connected computer.

## Get started

For Anki **26.8.1 or later** on **Windows, macOS, and Linux**.
Requires a TaskHero account.

1. Install the add-on and restart Anki.
2. In TaskHero, open **Integrations & API → Anki** and generate an Anki token.
3. Open **Tools → TaskHero for Anki → Settings…** in Anki and paste the token.
4. Click **Create “Study Anki”**, or **Refresh habits** to choose an existing
   everyday habit with one repetition. Choose your reward settings and **Save**.

Use one connected computer per synced collection. Sync before connecting or
reconnecting: reviews already on your computer are excluded, but mobile reviews
arriving later can still count. Unfinished batches don't carry into the next
TaskHero day.

## Privacy and help

Your cards, answers, notes, and deck names stay in Anki. No ads. TaskHero records
points earned and rewarded habit completions to understand integration usage.
